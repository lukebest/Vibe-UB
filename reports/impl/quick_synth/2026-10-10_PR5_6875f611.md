## Anomalies

- **`ub_dll_bcrc` / `ub_dll_bcrc_check`:** worst setup slack **−21.630 ns / −21.669 ns** at 12.412 ns on this Sky130 hd tt proxy. Logic depth 75 / 78; data arrival 33.918 / 33.965 ns. The leaves CRC a 160-bit flit in one `core_clk` (unrolled byte/bit loop). No latch, no inferred memory, no comb loop. HOOKS numbers match PRODUCT (SPEC §10 lists no hook ports on these leaves).
- **Lane dist/dedist:** 0 std cells at `NUM_LANES=4` and `8`. Yosys `proc; opt` is also 0 cells — pure wiring permute (SPEC 0-cycle). Not a unused-logic sweep.
- **`ub_rst_sync`:** whitelist handwritten SV; 2 async-reset flops; slack +11.955 ns. Included as a normal leaf.
- Scrambler/descrambler used labeled OPEN §13 `chparam` only on the Yosys command line: `SCR_TAPS=23'h2`, `LFSR_INIT=23'h5`, `SEED_MAP` slot k=k+3. Not product defaults.
